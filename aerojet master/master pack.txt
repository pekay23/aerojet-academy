2) CONVERSION FUNNEL (AUTHORITATIVE)

Global rules

Registration Fee is mandatory for all programmes.

Registration Fee = GHS 350 (one-time).

Payment method: Bank transfer + proof upload (no cards assumed).

Registration fee must be paid before the online application link is released.

After approval, issue a Confirmation Invoice per chosen programme’s rules.

NEXT STEPS (USE VERBATIM ON SITE)

Step 1: Registration Invoice
We will first issue an invoice for the GHS 350 registration fee. Payment of the registration fee is required before you can complete the online application.

Step 2: Online Application Form
Once payment is received and you send us confirmation, we will provide a link to complete the online application form for Academy review and approval.

Step 3: Confirmation Invoice
Upon approval, we will issue an invoice for the Confirmation payment applicable to your selected training program. Once paid, your seat is secured (or access is activated for exam-only/modular candidates).

Step 4: Student Portal Onboarding & Start Date / Access Confirmation
After confirmation, you will be onboarded onto our student portal. You will receive confirmation of the start date as per the conditions of your particular course or receive a link to access your learning materials for exam only and modular course candidates.

No aptitude tests on public site for now. Don’t claim medical/background checks unless enabled later.
