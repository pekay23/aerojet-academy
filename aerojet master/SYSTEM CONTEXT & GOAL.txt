SYSTEM CONTEXT & GOAL
I am building an Aviation Training Student Portal/LMS. The course structure is inspired by Aerojet Aviation's model, utilizing the EASA Part-66 certification framework for core engineering.
The system requires a specific Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), Course Enrollment, and Payment/Access architecture. The platform delivers courses through distinct "Pathways" (Roles). A student's chosen Pathway dictates their auto-enrollments, what they can see in the course catalog, what they pay for, and whether they get access to tuition/materials or just exams.

2. STUDENT PATHWAYS, ACCESS & ENROLLMENT LOGIC (User Roles)
When a student registers under "Aircraft Engineering", they must be assigned to one of the following Pathways.
Universal Rule: For all courses across all pathways, once a student has successfully paid, they are granted access to the respective course materials/tuition and exams (unless restricted by the "Exam Only" rule below).
Pathway 1 & 2: Full-Time Programs (4-Year and 2-Year)
 * Action: System auto-enrolls them into all required EASA Modules (1-17) over their respective schedules.
 * Payment & Access: Students pay for both tuition and exams. Upon payment, they get full access to course materials, tuition, and the exams.
 * UI Visibility: They MUST NOT see individual EASA modules in the available course catalog to purchase separately. They should only see their assigned dashboard and any available "Skilled Short Courses".
Pathway 3: Military Route (1-Year Full-Time)
 * Action: System auto-enrolls them into all required EASA Modules (1-17) on an accelerated 1-year schedule.
 * Payment & Access: Students pay for both tuition and exams. Upon payment, they get full access to course materials, tuition, and the exams.
 * UI Visibility: Same as the Full-Time programs. Individual modules are hidden from the catalog; they just follow their auto-enrolled timeline.
Pathway 4: Modular Route
 * Action: NO auto-enrollment in EASA modules.
 * Payment & Access: Students pay for specific modules à la carte. They pay for BOTH tuition and exams for the modules they select. Just like full-time students, upon payment, they gain full access to the course materials, tuition, and exams for their chosen modules.
 * UI Visibility: They MUST see the full catalog of individual EASA Modules under "Aircraft Engineering". They can freely select, pay for, and enroll in modules one at a time.
Pathway 5: Exam-Only Route
 * Action: NO auto-enrollment.
 * Payment & Access: Students pay strictly for exams. They DO NOT get access to course materials or tuition.
 * UI Visibility: They MUST see a specific variation of the catalog: "Exam-Only" versions of Modules 1-17 under "Aircraft Engineering". They cannot access study materials or coursework, only the exam booking/payment portals.
