MOD 01
Mathematics
Arithmetic · Algebra · Geometry
Covers the essential mathematical foundation for all subsequent technical modules. Topics include arithmetic operations, fractions, decimals, percentages, algebra, logarithms, geometry, trigonometry, and co-ordinate geometry. Required by all licence categories.
ArithmeticAlgebraGeometryTrigonometryLogarithmsStatistics
Prerequisites
None — first module in the sequence. Secondary school mathematics recommended.

Physics
Matter · Mechanics · Thermodynamics · Optics

MOD 03
Electrical Fundamentals
DC/AC Theory · Circuits · Motors & Generators
Comprehensive coverage of electrical theory from electron theory and static electricity through to complex AC circuits. Includes DC and AC sources, resistance, capacitance, inductance, transformers, filters, and motor/generator theory. Critical for both mechanical (B1) and avionics (B2) pathways.
DC CircuitsAC TheoryCapacitanceMagnetismInductanceTransformersMotors
Prerequisites
Modules 1 & 2 — electrical calculations require math (M1) and physics concepts like electron theory and magnetism (M2).

Builds on Module 3 to cover semiconductor devices (diodes, transistors, integrated circuits), printed circuit boards, and servomechanisms (synchros). B2 licence requires deeper knowledge than B1. Not required for Category A. Foundational for understanding modern aircraft electronics.
DiodesTransistorsIntegrated CircuitsPCBsSynchrosServomechanisms

MOD 05
Digital Techniques
Electronic Instrument Systems · Avionics · Data Buses
Covers the digital and avionics environment essential for modern aircraft maintenance. Topics include numbering systems, data conversion, logic circuits, microprocessors, data buses (ARINC 429, ARINC 629), multiplexing, fibre optics, electronic displays (EFiS), software management, and ESD precautions. B2 exam is significantly more extensive.
Logic CircuitsMicroprocessorsData BusesEFISFibre OpticsESDSoftware Mgmt
Prerequisites
Modules 3 & 4 — digital circuits build on electrical fundamentals and semiconductor theory. Module 1 (binary/hex numbering systems).

AlloysCompositesCorrosionFastenersBearingsControl CablesPipes

MOD 07
Maintenance Practices
Safety · Tools · Inspection · Documentation
The core practical module covering the day-to-day activities of aircraft maintenance. Includes safety precautions, workshop practices, tools, test equipment, engineering drawings, fits and clearances, riveting, welding, aircraft weight and balance, handling/storage, disassembly/inspection/repair, and maintenance procedures. Updated June 2024 — essay component removed.
SafetyTools & EquipmentRivetingWeldingWeight & BalanceInspectionsDocumentation
Prerequisites
Modules 1, 2, 6 — maintenance calculations (weight/balance) use math (M1), physical principles inform techniques (M2), and material knowledge (M6) is applied throughout.

MOD 08
Basic Aerodynamics
Atmosphere · Lift & Drag · Flight Stability
Builds on Module 2 physics to explain the principles of flight. Covers the International Standard Atmosphere (ISA), airflow over aerofoils, generation of lift and drag, theory of flight, high-speed flight (compressibility), and aircraft stability and control (static and dynamic). Essential groundwork for the aircraft-specific modules (11–13).
ISAAerofoilsLift & DragTheory of FlightCompressibilityStability
Prerequisites
Module 2 (Physics) — aerodynamics directly applies fluid mechanics, Bernoulli's theorem, and gas laws from the physics module.

MOD 09
Human Factors
Performance · Error · CRM · Safety Culture
Examines the human element in aviation maintenance safety. Covers human performance and limitations, social psychology, team dynamics and communication (CRM), workload, fatigue, stress, situational awareness, and maintenance error. Critically studies accident causation models. Essay requirement removed June 2024 — MCQ only.
Human PerformanceError ModelsCRMFatigueStressSafety Culture
Prerequisites
None formally required — though Module 7 (Maintenance Practices) context helps apply human factors principles in real maintenance scenarios.

Aviation Legislation
EASA · Part-M · Part-145 · Part-66
Covers the regulatory framework governing aviation maintenance. Topics include the role of ICAO and EASA, certifying staff regulations (Part-66), approved maintenance organisations (Part-145/CAMO), commercial air transport operations, aircraft certification, Part-M airworthiness, and international requirements. Essay requirement removed June 2024.
ICAOEASAPart-66Part-145Part-M / CAMOAircraft Certification

MOD 11A
Turbine Aeroplane Aerodynamics, Structures & Systems
Fixed-wing Turbine · Cat A1/A3 · B1.1/B1.3
The most extensive B1 module — covers all major systems of turbine-powered aeroplanes in detail. Includes advanced theory of flight, airframe structures (fuselage, wings, empennage), air conditioning & pressurisation, instruments/avionics, electrical power, flight controls, fuel systems, hydraulics, ice protection, landing gear, oxygen, pneumatics, and onboard maintenance systems (ATA chapters 21–45).
Theory of FlightAirframe StructuresPressurisationFlight ControlsHydraulicsFuel SystemsLanding GearOBMS
Prerequisites
Modules 1–8 form the foundation — especially M2 (physics), M3 (electrical), M5 (digital/avionics), M6 (materials), M7 (maintenance practices), and M8 (aerodynamics).

MOD 12
Helicopter Aerodynamics, Structures & Systems
Rotary Wing · Cat A3/A4 · B1.3/B1.4
Helicopter-specific module covering rotary-wing aerodynamics, hover, climb, descent, and autorotation theory. Includes rotor systems (blade tracking, vibration), transmission (gearboxes, clutches), flight control systems (collective, cyclic, anti-torque), and all major helicopter systems: air conditioning, electrical power, fuel, hydraulics, ice protection, landing gear, and instruments.
Rotary AerodynamicsRotor SystemsTransmissionCyclic/CollectiveAnti-torqueAutorotationVibration Analysis

Modules 1–8 are required foundations. Module 8 (Basic Aerodynamics) is especially important as rotary-wing aerodynamics extends fixed-wing principles significantly.

MOD 13
Aircraft Aerodynamics, Structures & Systems
Avionics Focus · Cat B2 Only
The B2-specific equivalent of Module 11. Provides avionics engineers with the comprehensive knowledge of aircraft systems they need to maintain and troubleshoot avionics, electrical systems, and instruments. Includes flight control surfaces, auto-flight (autopilot, autothrust), communications, navigation, electrical power, fuel systems, fire detection, landing gear, pressurisation, and IMA (Integrated Modular Avionics). Updated June 2024.
Autopilot / AFCSCommunicationsNavigationElectrical PowerIMAEFIS/ECAMGlass Cockpit
Prerequisites
Modules 1–5, 8 — particularly M3 (electrical), M4 (electronics), M5 (digital/avionics), and M8 (aerodynamics) all directly feed into M13 content.

Gas Turbine Engine
Turbofan · Turboprop · Turboshaft · APU
Comprehensive coverage of gas turbine engines for turbine-rated licences (A1/A3/B1.1/B1.3). Prerequisites

Piston Engine
Reciprocating Engines · Cat A2/A4 · B1.2/B1.4 · B3

Topics include engine fundamentals (2/4-stroke cycles, engine types), performance, engine construction, induction/exhaust/cooling systems, supercharging, lubrication systems, fuel systems and carburettors, ignition systems, engine starting, and engine monitoring. Required for piston-rated licence subcategories.
Engine CyclesEngine ConstructionCarburettorsMagneto IgnitionSuperchargingLubricationCooling

MOD 17
Propeller
Fixed & Variable Pitch · Governors · Synchronisation
Covers propeller theory, construction, and maintenance for piston and turboprop aircraft. Topics include propeller theory (blade angle, pitch, slip), propeller construction (fixed pitch, ground adjustable, variable pitch), propeller governor and control systems, synchronisation/synchrophasing, ice protection, propeller storage, and inspection/maintenance procedures.
Propeller TheoryFixed PitchVariable PitchGovernor SystemsSynchronisationIce Protection
Prerequisites
Modules 2 (physics — fluid mechanics, rotation), 6 (materials for propeller construction), 15 or 16 (engine knowledge). Also closely linked to Module 8 (aerodynamics).
