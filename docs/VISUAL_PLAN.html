Public Pages Visual Enhancement Plan

Professional, minimalist imagery for Aerojet Academy's public-facing pages

Direction: Muted tones, clean compositions, cool palette (blues/grays/whites), editorial/documentary photography. Non-color-blocking, non-saturated. Befitting a world-class EASA Approved Training Organisation.
