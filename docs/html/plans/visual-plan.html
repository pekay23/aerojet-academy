Public Pages Visual Enhancement Plan

Professional, minimalist imagery for Aerojet Academy's public-facing pages.

01 · Overview

Overview

Professional, minimalist imagery for Aerojet Academy's public-facing pages. Direction: muted tones, clean compositions, cool palette (blues/grays/whites), editorial/documentary photography style. Non-color-blocking, non-saturated. Befitting a world-class EASA Approved Training Organisation.

02 · Image Generation Settings (All images)

Image Generation Settings (All images)

Setting Value
Style Photorealistic, editorial/documentary photography
Lighting Natural, soft, diffused (no harsh flash)
Color grade Cool, desaturated, professional
People Predominantly Black African, diverse ages, natural poses
Composition Clean negative space for text overlays on heroes
Format .webp, quality 80
No Text, watermarks, logos, branding in generated images

A. HOMEPAGE HERO SLIDES (3 images)

A1. "The Hangar"

File: /images/hero/hero-slide1.webp

Wide-angle interior of a modern aircraft maintenance hangar at golden hour. A commercial narrow-body aircraft (similar to A320) is centered with engine cowlings open. Soft natural light streaming through large hangar doors on the left, creating long shadows on the polished concrete floor. Two engineers in clean navy coveralls and hi-vis vests inspecting the landing gear — small in frame to emphasize scale. Muted cool tones, slightly desaturated. Cinematic depth of field. Documentary photography style. Minimalist composition with negative space in the upper portion for text overlay. No logos or text visible.

A2. "Precision Training"

File: /images/hero/hero-slide2.webp

Clean, modern aviation training classroom viewed from a low angle. Rows of young Black African engineering students in matching academy polo shirts, focused on technical drawings at their desks. Large digital screen at front showing aircraft cross-section schematics. Soft diffused overhead lighting. Cool neutral tones — white walls, light wood desks, subtle blue accents. Shallow depth of field with foreground student sharp and background gently blurred. Professional editorial photography. Calm, studious atmosphere. Natural skin tones. Upper third of frame has clean negative space.

A3. "Hands-On Excellence"

File: /images/hero/hero-slide3.webp

Close-up to medium shot of a young Black African female engineering student performing avionics wiring inspection inside an aircraft cockpit panel. She wears safety glasses and clean blue coveralls with subtle academy patch. Fiber optic inspection light illuminating intricate wire harnesses in warm amber. Background blurred showing more of the avionics bay. Cool ambient light contrasts with warm task light. Tightly composed, cinematic. Documentary style. Muted palette — navy, charcoal, amber highlights. Professional and aspirational. No text or logos.
