Design

02 · Color tokens

The Aerojet palette is split into three layers, all defined in app/globals.css and exported as Tailwind utilities through tailwind.config.ts…

  1. shadcn/ui semantic palette (the portal default) — comes from the slate base, with a .dark class on <html> to invert.
  2. Aerojet brand palette — six --aero-* CSS variables used on the public marketing surface.
  3. Public-site palette — three --public-* variables used in the nav, footer, and CTA buttons.

There are also a handful of hard-coded hex surfaces used on the live homepage and in design-lab variants.
