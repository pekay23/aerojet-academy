05 · RULES

Don't

  • No emoji as icons. Always Lucide.
  • No raw bg-blue-500 on the public site — use --aero-*.
  • No buttons smaller than 44×44 px (WCAG).
  • No font-bold on body — font-medium (500) is the heading cap.
