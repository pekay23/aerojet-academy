Voice & visual language

Three canonical "looks" coexist in the repo — the editorial Variant C (paper background, blue ink) is the live public site, while the design-lab variants A and B (dark, technical cockpit) and D / E (modern flat marketing) serve as A/B comparison material. All brand blues are deliberately darkened from their HSL prototypes to meet WCAG AA 4.5:1 contrast against white.

Focus & accessibility

A11y motion rules

All interactive elements get a visible focus ring (2 px, 2 px offset) using focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-ring focus-visible:ring-offset-2. The skip-link at the top of every page is sr-only focus:not-sr-only and lands at inset-4 z-50 rounded-lg bg-white px-4 py-2 font-bold shadow-lg. body.exam-lockdown is a hard mode used during exams that strips the sidebar, sticky elements, breadcrumb, and registration banners.

08 Â· RULES

Do & don't

Hard rules from the codebase and the design gap audit. Violating these typically causes a code review rejection or a "design system debt" entry in docs/audits/.

do
  • Use bg-aerojet-blue on the public site, bg-primary inside the portal.
  • Compose pages from components/marketing/sections/*.
  • Reach for the font-sans / font-serif / font-outfit aliases, not raw family names.
  • Use focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-ring on every interactive element.
  • Keep buttons at 44Ã—44 px minimum.
  • Use Lucide ArrowRight for CTAs.
  • Reach for the 4-pt spacing scale â€” p-2 â€¦ py-32.
don't
  • No emoji as icons. Always Lucide.
  • No raw bg-blue-500 / bg-red-500 on the public site â€” use --aero-*.
  • No text-white inside portal chrome â€” use text-card-foreground.
  • No buttons smaller than 44Ã—44 px (WCAG).
  • No transition-all on heavy properties â€” transition-colors or transition-transform.
  • No default font-bold on body â€” font-medium (500) is the heading cap.
  • No emoji as bullet markers.
