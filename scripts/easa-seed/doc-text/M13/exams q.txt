How are composite materials protected from lightening strikes?
Composite materials are protected using:
• Woven aluminium mesh
• Aluminium foil sheets
• Aluminised paint
• Flame-sprayed aluminium
• Bonding metal parts to composite surfaces
Are HF air to air and air to ground ?
Yes, HF (High Frequency) communication is used for both air-to-air and air-to-ground
due to its long-range capability via ionospheric reflection
Under water locator beacon is attached to what ?
The ULB is attached to the front panel of the flight data recorder (FDR)
What triggers the autoslat system?
The autoslat system is triggered by stall warning conditions, when the aircraft
approaches critical angle of attack
What does cyclic and collective control ?
Cyclic control: Changes the tilt of the rotor disc, controlling movement forward,
backward, and sideways.
Collective control: Changes pitch of all rotor blades simultaneously, controlling
vertical movement (lift)
What frequency uses 30khz - 300khz
This range is categorized as Low Frequency (LF)

Autopilot adjusts the center of gravity by transferring fuel between main tanks and

What does audio integration system consist of ?
Audio Selection Panels (ACP)

Auto circuit breakers ...are they AC or DC or both?
Auto circuit breakers are used in both AC and DC systems for circuit protection
Difference between fuse and current limiter ?
Fuse = fast-acting, low power, sensitive to quick surges.
Current limiter = slow-acting, heavy-duty, tolerates short surges.
What happens to engines at high altitude? What happens to density?
Engines: Suffer performance loss due to lower oxygen levels.
Density: Decreases with altitude, reducing lift and engine efficiency
What structure runs along the length of the wing ?
The main spar runs along the length of the wing, serving as its primary load-bearing
structure
What battery does emergency transmitter(ELT) use ?
The ELT uses an internal battery pack designed to last at least 48 hours after
activation. It is sealed and must be replaced according to the manufacturer’s interval

What does yaw damper control?
The yaw damper controls Dutch roll damping, turn coordination, and engine failure
compensation by automatically adjusting the rudder based on inputs from a Yaw Rate
Gyro and other sensors
Purpose of yaw gyro?
The yaw gyro senses yaw rate (rotations about the vertical axis) and provides signals to
the rudder servo for damping Dutch roll and stabilizing the aircraft
Why does gyro have tendencies to be unstable/ drift?
Gyros drift due to the rotation of the Earth, mechanical imperfections, and friction in
the gyro bearings. This apparent movement causes the gyro to slowly lose directional
reference over time
What is profile navigation and Lateral navigation ?
 Lateral navigation controls the aircraft’s heading or track across the ground.
 Profile navigation manages vertical flight, including climb, descent, altitude hold,
and thrust management
What length of antenna is most effective in relation to the frequency ?
The most effective antenna length is one half the wavelength of the operating
frequency
Avionics world vs open world?
In avionics, systems operate in a closed (controlled) world with certified and reliable
hardware/software. The open world refers to generic computing systems where such
strict certification is not required
What kind of lights are no collision lights?
Anti-collision lights include red/white rotating beacons and strobe lights used to
make the aircraft more visible to others

How are toilets flushed? Manually? Electrically? Pneumatically?
Aircraft toilets are flushed electrically, pneumatically, or manually, depending on
system design. Most modern systems use electric vacuum systems
How is water supplied to galleys and toilets? Gravity? Pneumatic pump? Electric motor?
Water is supplied by pumps—typically electric or pneumatic. Gravity systems are

When do you use rain repellant? On dry windscreen? On wet windscreen? On wet or dry
windscreen?
Rain repellent is used on a wet windscreen, not dry. It helps disperse rainwater more

Where is water separator located in the air cycle machine?
The water separator is located after the air cycle machine’s turbine outlet, where it
removes moisture from cooled air
How many fire extinguishers are required for 150 passengers?
A minimum of 3 portable fire extinguishers is required for 150 passengers, based on
standard aviation safety requirements
How does the pilot engage Go around?
The pilot engages Go-Around (GA) mode by pressing the TO/GA switch (Take-Off/Go-
Around), usually on the throttle lever. This commands the autopilot or autothrust to
climb. On other aircraft types the throttles must be manually moved to fully forward
position.

How is defueling done? Through suction and booster pump
Defueling can be done by suction or using the aircraft's booster pumps in reverse
mode
What could cause the aircraft to fly outside the flight envelope ? Fault with egpws? Fault
with Autopilot? Input of wrong CG?
Possible causes:
• Incorrect CG input
• Autopilot malfunction
• Aircraft Weight Computation
• Aft Centre of Gravity out of tolerated limit
• Alpha Floor Acquisition Wind Shear conditions
• Operational Speed Computation
• Lateral Asymmetry due of engine failure
• Not typically EGPWS fault, since it’s warning-only
Difference between GPWS and EGPWS?
 GPWS uses barometric and radio altimeter data to detect terrain.
 EGPWS enhances GPWS with GPS and terrain databases to predict terrain threats
Role of thermal relief valve in hydraulics ?
It protects the system from overpressure due to fluid expansion, especially from
temperature increase
What is class A cargo? (Page 824)
Class A cargo compartments are only accessible during flight and designed to contain
a fire without detection or extinguishing—limited to small, low-risk items
Role of safety relief valves in cabin pressurization system?

--- Page 6 ---
They prevent overpressure in the cabin by venting excess air if the cabin pressure
exceeds safe limits
What makes up the IDG? CDU + AC generator
The Integrated Drive Generator (IDG) consists of a Constant Speed Drive Unit (CDU)
and an AC Generator
What is power up test?
A Power-Up Test checks the functionality of avionics systems and sensors during
aircraft start-up
How is VSI measured?
The Vertical Speed Indicator (VSI) uses static pressure changes over time to
calculate climb or descent rate
What does RVDT do?
A Rotary Variable Differential Transformer (RVDT) converts angular displacement into
an electrical signal for systems like control surface position sensors
What are the types of aircraft instruments?
Pressure instruments (Altimeter, ASI, VSI)
Gyroscopic instruments (Attitude, Heading, Turn Coordinator)
Magnetic instruments (Compass)
Advantage of fly by optics over fly by wire ?
Fly-by-Optics uses fiber optic cables, which are immune to EMI and faster.
Fly-by-Wire uses electrical signals, which are susceptible to interference but well
established

Difference between static and rotary inverter ?
Static inverter: No moving parts, more reliable, lighter.
Rotary inverter: Uses a motor-generator, heavier, older technology
What is done to fire extingushers periodically? Weighed? Pressure checked?
They are periodically weighed to check for loss of contents and pressure-checked to

Detected using systems like Radar, Inertial Navigation, and Angle of Attack Sensors,
